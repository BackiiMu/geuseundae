# 공통 흉가 환경 아트

승인된 `assets/maps/haunted-courtyard-v2.png`를 시각 기준으로 삼아 내장 이미지 생성 도구로 16개 에셋 시트를 제작했다.

최종 이미지: `godot/assets/environment/haunted-village-atlas.png`

참조 영역: `godot/assets/environment/atlas-regions.json`. 투명 여백을 제외한 영역을 엔진에서 직접 참조한다. 원본 이미지 파일은 잘라서 변경하지 않는다.

적용: `godot/scripts/room_view.gd`에서 모든 방 테마가 같은 에셋을 조합한다. 돌바닥, 담장, 기와, 목재 발판, 창호, 기둥, 포탈, 소나무, 대문, 우물, 신당, 상자, 옹기, 부적, 등불.

검수: Godot 그리기 명령을 내보내 브라우저 뷰어로 아트 조합을 확인했다. 실제 게임의 GPU 출력 스크린샷과는 구분한다. 이동 및 포탈 물리 검사도 다시 실행했다. 실행 중인 작은 게임 창은 그대로 두었으며 다음 게임 실행에 적용된다.

## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game environment sprite atlas for Godot, EXACT 4 by 4 regular grid of 16 equal square cells in a square image, genuinely transparent background. Reference image is the approved visual style: Korean haunted hanok village, chunky low resolution square pixel clusters, dark desaturated indigo and gray stone, muted jade moss, weathered brown wood, sparse amber lanterns. Match this style closely, NOT placeholders or smooth drawings. NO grid lines, NO labels, NO text, NO characters. Each asset contained entirely in its own cell with clear transparent margin around props. Grid positions are mandatory, reading left to right top to bottom. Row 1: (0) seamless horizontal moss-covered stone floor TOP strip, top surface at very top of cell, layered cracked dark stone below fills entire cell with opaque pixels, edge-to-edge; (1) opaque dark stone/soil FILL texture covering cell edge-to-edge; (2) opaque Korean cracked white-gray plaster courtyard WALL with stone foundation covering whole cell, no roof; (3) thin wooden walkway platform strip at TOP of transparent cell, horizontal planks with moss and dark timber beam directly underneath, strip occupies only top 1/4 of cell. Row 2: (4) opaque hanok wooden facade bay with lattice paper windows, aged brown beams, covering whole cell, NO roof; (5) a broad traditional gray curved Korean tiled roof, free-standing sprite with transparent space below, centered and nearly filling cell width, most roof mass in top half of cell; (6) vertical aged wooden support pillar sprite; (7) tall open doorway portal with traditional timber frame, dark empty opening, small stone threshold, no glow, no roof. Row 3: (8) large crooked Korean pine tree full silhouette, jade angular leaf masses and dark bent trunk; (9) ruined Korean tiled entrance gate with broken wooden doors; (10) old stone well with wooden crossbeam rope and bucket; (11) small Korean roadside shrine, gray tiled small roof and dark wooden interior. Row 4: (12) stack of old wooden crates; (13) cluster of traditional brown onggi jars; (14) three faded red paper talismans hanging from a thin rope; (15) a single hanging warm amber hanok lantern. Uniform orthographic side-view, consistent chunky pixel density, 2-3 shade steps per material, crisp edges, no blur, no cast shadows outside asset silhouette, no background scenery, no checkerboard baked into transparency. All sixteen cells have exactly equal bounds, strict 4x4 sprite sheet. Props each isolated within its assigned cell and not touching adjacent cells. Tile cells 0,1,2,4 are opaque edge-to-edge squares suitable for repeating. No UI.
